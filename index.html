Fan and AI game port takedowns

An open dataset of fan-made and AI-assisted ports of commercial video games (browser ports, PC ports, recompilations and decompilations) that were taken down after a copyright claim. It is the data behind the PortedGames takedown tracker, where every row is listed with its evidence. Updated daily.

Use it

// JavaScript
const rows = await (await fetch('https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wangxiaoxiang5599/fan-port-takedowns/main/data/takedowns.json')).json();
const byHolder = Object.groupBy(rows, (r) => r.rightsHolder);
# Python
import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_csv('https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wangxiaoxiang5599/fan-port-takedowns/main/data/takedowns.csv', parse_dates=['live', 'takenDown'])
df.groupby('rightsHolder').size().sort_values(ascending=False)

Each row has the port, the original game, the rights holder, the kind of port, how it was taken down, the release and takedown dates, days online, a short note and the sources. Field descriptions are in the README.

Scope

A port gets a row only with evidence of a copyright claim: the notice, the author's post, or a notice on the port's own site. Ports their authors closed on their own, ports that are just offline, and emulators or piracy tools are not counted. No game files, ROMs or ISOs are hosted or linked. Know of a missing takedown? Open an issue with a source.
